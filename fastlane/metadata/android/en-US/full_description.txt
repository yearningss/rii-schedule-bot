Mobile application providing schedule of classes and bell timetable for students and lecturers of Rubtsovsk Industrial Institute (RII AltSTU).

Features:
* View schedule for all academic groups online and offline.
* Automatic offline caching for instant access without internet.
* Week parity detection (Week I / Week II).
* Subgroup selection for laboratory work.
* Native desktop widgets for quick access to upcoming lectures.
* 12 dynamic seasonal themes and launcher icon customization.
* Exam timetable and bell schedule.
* Privacy-focused: zero ads, zero trackers, open source MIT license.
